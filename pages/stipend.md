### Travel Stpiend

Stipends up to $1850 are open to undergraduate students, graduate students, and early career post-doctoral researchers. To be eligible, you must attend the entire workshop in person, be affiliated with a US institution at the time of the workshop, and be traveling domestically. These stipends are intended to help increase the participation in planetary data usage, creation, and management by promising and highly talented students and early career researchers who would greatly benefit from in-person training but lack funding for travel. These stipends thus increase the overall quality of the planetary science workforce.

[The travel stipend application is available here.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScE4MJUrrKN3LWr_suBtY1_FWWvJgiQ0OTmmUUStOIhXO7Axw/viewform)

As part of this application, you will need to provide a brief personal statement. The statement must not exceed 3000 characters (including spaces), and should describe your interest in this workshop, how it will impact and advance your career, and why you require funding in order to attend. This statement must be submitted within this form, NOT emailed separately.

This application also requires at least one confidential letter of recommendation, with no more than one optional additional letter. This form requires you to provide the name(s) and email address(es) of your recommender(s), and provides instructions on how recommenders must submit their letter.

Stipends are funded as part of this conference through NASA's Topical Workshops, Symposia, and Conferences (TWSC) program, and are managed by Cornell University and its Spacecraft Planetary Image Facility (SPIF).

If you have any questions regarding this travel grant or application, please contact Zoe Learner Ponterio at spif@cornell.edu.
